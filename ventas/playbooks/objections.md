# Objection matrix

Answers are drafts for a human to adapt and send. Never invent figures; use the clinic's own numbers. Never reveal promotional pricing in response to a cold objection.

| Objection | What the doctor really fears | Consultative answer (draft) |
|---|---|---|
| "I like the human touch; bots are cold." | A clumsy bot will put off demanding patients. | "Agreed, trust is everything in a clinic. The assistant does not replace your team: it covers the first minute (hours, services, basic qualification) and hands the chat to a person as soon as the patient asks for one, with the context already captured." |
| "What if it gives a wrong price or medical advice?" | The AI hallucinates or commits to fees or clinical statements. | "It is limited to your approved catalog and fixed rules. Anything outside it (clinical questions, quotes it does not have) is handed to your team. It never diagnoses." |
| "It sounds expensive to just try." | No clear return on investment. | "Fair. Let's use your numbers: if the pilot rescues one high-ticket patient who wrote outside office hours, the pilot is paid back. Payment is 50 percent to start and 50 percent when you see it working." |
| "I already use WhatsApp Business quick replies." | Shortcuts already solve it. | "Quick replies still need someone to type while attending a patient. The assistant keeps the conversation going in seconds and gives you the chat ready to close." |
| "Is it safe for my WhatsApp number?" | A ban of the clinic's only channel. | "Honest answer: unofficial bridges carry some ban risk. We limit volume and schedule, and we can use the official WhatsApp Business API if you prefer. If anything fails, the chat returns to your own app untouched." |
| "I need to think about it." | Unclear next step or a hidden objection. | "Of course. What is the one thing you would want to be sure about? I can send the one-page service order so you can review it calmly." |
| "Send me information." | Polite brush-off. | "Sure. Instead of a PDF, I can send a 60-second demo with your clinic's name; it shows more than a brochure." |
| "Not now / busy season." | Timing, no urgency. | "Understood. I will leave the door open. If you want, I note a date to check back, inside business hours." |
| "Who are you / how did you get my number?" | Privacy and legitimacy. | "I found your clinic's public business listing. If you prefer I do not write again, tell me and I will remove you from my list today." |
| (Silence after two touches) | None observable. | One last graceful-exit message, then archive. Never chase. |

## Rules

- Do not argue with a refusal. A "no" or "do not contact me" ends the sequence.
- Do not say "cheap". Sell the outcome (more replies, more appointments, fewer lost messages), not the technology.
- Do not mention n8n, models, webhooks or infrastructure to the prospect.
- Filter out prospects who haggle from the first message or ask for unlimited custom work at the entry price.
